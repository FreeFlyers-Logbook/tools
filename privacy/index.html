Free Flyers Logbook

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Free Flyers Logbook (the "App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and stores information when you use our Android application. By using the App you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information you provide directly:

a) Account Information

  • Email address, used to create and manage your account via Firebase Authentication.

b) User-Generated Content

  • Flight log entries (dates, sites, duration, notes, and related details)
  • Glider records (name, model, purchase date, and related details)
  • Site records (name, location type, and related details)
  • Photos you choose to upload in connection with flights or gliders

We do not collect your name, phone number, physical address, payment information, or device location.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide the App's features:

  • To create and authenticate your account
  • To store and display your flight logs, glider records, and site records
  • To allow you to retrieve your data across devices

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing, or any purpose beyond operating the App.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase services (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Storage), hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure. Data may be stored on servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States.

Google's privacy practices are described at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Security measures include:

  • All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Firebase Authentication ensures only you can access your account
  • Firestore security rules enforce that each user can only read and write their own data
  • Firebase Storage rules ensure uploaded photos are only accessible to the account that uploaded them
  • Firebase infrastructure is maintained and monitored by Google, which holds internationally recognised security certifications including ISO 27001 and SOC 2

While no system can guarantee absolute security, your data is protected by industry-standard cloud security practices.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is not used for advertising purposes. We do not use any third-party analytics or crash reporting tools.

The only third party that processes your data is Google Firebase, as the infrastructure provider for the App.

7. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data directly within the App via the account settings, or by contacting us at the address below.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your country of residence, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. Account deletion can be performed directly within the App. To exercise other rights, please contact us by email.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
